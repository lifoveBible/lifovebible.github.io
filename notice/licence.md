Changes made for Lifove Bible:

- Converted from the eBible.org SWORD module (nya2026eb, source files dated 2025-05-20)
  to the Lifove Bible .lfa format (one verse per line, plain text).
- Section headings, paragraph and poetry layout removed; Psalm titles kept at the start of verse 1.
- Verse numbering changed from NRSV to KJV versification:
  3 John 1:15 joined to 1:14; Revelation 12:18 joined to the start of 13:1.
- Tables flattened into a single line per verse (cells separated by commas, rows by semicolons).
