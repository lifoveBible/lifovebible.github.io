## Version 7.5.2 is coming soon! (Under review)

- New app icon: a book with a bookmark
- Bookmarks, History, My Highlights, My Memos and Reading Statistics now look like the reading screen (they follow its background color, text color and text size)
- Search in My Memos: results narrow as you type, matching the memo text and the verse location (such as the book name)
- My Highlights shows each verse in your highlight style (underline, highlighter or text color)
- Reading Statistics shows progress bars
- Fixed multi-line memos showing only their first line in the list
- Fixed My Memos ignoring the menu button position setting
- The FAQ is updated from the server without an app update
- New open-book icon for the audio notification
- Fixed the audio notification not appearing in one-column view on Android 8 or later
- 'Status/Title Bars' is disabled while Full screen reading is on (Android 11 or later)
- Fixed text-to-speech in two-column comparison reading other versions in the left column with the first version's voice: only the first version is read now
- Auto scroll: shorter wait on a new chapter (it now waits until you've read down to where text-to-speech places the current verse, instead of a third of the screen)
- Auto scroll now starts at 30 lines per minute by default (a speed you already used is kept)
- Auto scroll keeps its lines-per-minute speed when you pinch to change the text size

## New commentaries and cross-references

You can now download the following English commentaries and cross-reference data from Menu (≡) > Settings > Data Management > Bible Data Download, and read them alongside the Bible text in the Parallel Viewer.

- Barnes’ Notes (NT)
- Clarke’s Commentary
- Calvin’s Commentaries
- Treasury of Scripture Knowledge
- Geneva Bible Notes (1599)
- Catena Aurea (Aquinas) (Gospels)
- Robertson’s Word Pictures (NT)

## Version 7.5.1

Lifove Bible works again on the latest Android (15, 16).
You can bring in your old Bible data and memos with Menu (≡) > Settings > Data Management > **Connect Previous Data Folder**.

If you have any problem, please let us know with 'Report Issues' in the menu.
