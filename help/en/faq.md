## Getting started

### How do I get Bible data?

Go to Menu (≡) > Settings > Data Management > Bible Data Download and pick the translations you need.
Only translations with copyright permission are provided.
Then choose a translation from 'Single Viewer' in the menu to read it.

### Can I add Bible data that isn't in the download list?

Yes. Lifove Bible is a viewer for .lfa files and the older Bethlehem Bible data (.bdf). You can also search the web for "Lifove Bible data download".
After downloading (unzip it first if it is a zip file), create a folder on your phone and save the .lfa file in it.
Then go to Menu (≡) > Settings > Data Management > **Connect Previous Data Folder** and select that folder to install the data into the app.

### Can I use the Korean Revised Version (개역개정)?

Lifove Bible is a free app that makes no profit, so it does not sell Bible data. If you previously bought the file (kornkrv_hs.lfa) from a third party, install it like this:

- Create a folder on your phone (for example, bible), create a **restrict** folder inside it, and put kornkrv_hs.lfa there. If the file is anywhere other than the restrict folder, it shows in the list but will not open.
- Go to Menu (≡) > Settings > Data Management > **Connect Previous Data Folder** and select the folder above restrict (for example, bible), not restrict itself.

### How do I bring in my old Bible data (including mp3) and memos?

Go to Menu (≡) > Settings > Data Management > **Connect Previous Data Folder** and select your old bible folder.

- Bible texts (.lfa, .bdf) and memo/highlight backups are imported into the app. You can choose to import only the files, or the memo/highlight backups as well.
- Audio (mp3) bought from a third party must be in the mp3 folder inside your Bible data folder, and it is played directly from there.

### How do I back up and restore my data?

Menu (≡) > Settings > Data Management > Data Backup saves to the 'Download/LifoveBible' folder, which is kept even if the app is uninstalled.
To restore, use Restore Data and pick the backup file.
Please back up before changing phones or uninstalling the app.

### The app icon has changed

Starting with 7.5.2, the app icon is a book with a bookmark. The name is still Lifove Bible, so if you can't find it on your home screen, search for it by name.

## Reading

### How do I move around quickly?

- Swipe left for the next chapter and right for the previous one, like turning pages.
- Tap the book, chapter, or verse button in the viewer to jump directly.
- Use the navigation buttons on the right for the next chapter, one screen, or previously visited chapters. (Viewer Settings > Use Navigation Bar)
- Volume keys can move by a verse, a screen, or a chapter. (Viewer Settings > Volume Key)

### Auto scroll doesn't seem to start

- After you tap Menu (≡) > **Start Auto Scroll**, it waits as long as it takes to read down to the reading line near the top of the screen, then starts moving. The faster the speed (lines per minute) and the larger the text, the shorter the wait.
- Touching the screen pauses it; it starts again 2 seconds later.
- With Menu (≡) > Settings > Viewer Settings > **Auto scroll to next chapter** turned on, it waits at the end of a chapter for the time it takes to read what is left on the screen, then moves to the next chapter. Short chapters that need no scrolling also stay for their reading time before moving on.
- Auto scroll can't run while a chapter is being read aloud (▶︎). Both move the screen, so starting one stops the other.

### How do I change the text size and colors?

- Pinch with two fingers in the viewer to change the text size.
- Viewer Settings lets you change the text color, background color, bold text, verse spacing, and line spacing.
- Night Mode makes the buttons follow the viewer colors to reduce glare at night.

### How do I move the book/chapter bar to the bottom or use more of the screen?

- Turn on Viewer Settings > **Book/chapter bar at bottom**.
- Turn on Viewer Settings > **Full screen reading** to hide the status and navigation bars while you scroll down. Scroll up a little to bring them back. (Android 11 or later)
- While Full screen reading is on, 'Status/Title Bars' is not used and appears grayed out. (7.5.2 or later)

### How do I mark chapters as read?

- Tap the checkbox on the book/chapter bar. Read marks are kept separately for each translation.
- With Viewer Settings > Auto Read Check, the chapter is marked when you move to the next one with the volume keys or navigation buttons.
- See Read Statistics in the menu for statistics by translation.

### How do I use bookmarks and history?

- Use 'Add Bookmark' in the menu to save your place. Long-press a bookmark to delete it.
- History keeps the places you read recently. The ↶ ↷ buttons in the viewer take you back to places you viewed before.

## Listening

### How do I listen to a chapter?

Tap the **▶︎** button in the viewer.
Chapters without an audio file are read by your phone's text-to-speech (TTS), and the view follows the verse being read.
While playing, tap the button again to change the speed: 1.0x, 1.3x, 1.6x, 1.8x, 2.0x.
The first time text-to-speech prepares a chapter, it may take a while depending on your phone. While you listen, the next chapter is prepared in advance, so from the next chapter on it plays without delay.
In the Parallel Viewer, only the first translation is read. (Two-column view: 7.5.2 or later)

### I want a different voice

Go to Menu (≡) > Settings > **Speech (TTS) Settings** to choose a voice (with preview) and install voice data if needed.
If the speech files take up too much storage, you can limit or clear the cache on the same screen.

### How do I hide the audio button?

Turn off Viewer Settings > Show audio button.

## Study

### How do I read several translations together?

Choose the translations in 'Parallel Viewer' from the menu. You need at least two translations downloaded.

- You can read in one or two columns. (Viewer Settings > One Column Parallel Viewer)
- Change the order and colors in List Management > Bible Order and Color.
- Selecting many translations can make moving between chapters slower.

### How do I add memos and highlights?

- Long-press a verse to write a memo or highlight it from the menu that appears.
- Tap verses to select several, then highlight, copy, or send them together from the menu.
- Memo display, memo color, and highlight colors can be changed in Viewer Settings.
- See them all in My Highlight and My Memo from the menu.
- In My Memo, type in the search box at the top to narrow the list as you type. It searches both the memo text and the book name, and shows only memos that contain every word you enter. (7.5.2 or later)

### How do I look at another passage and come back?

In the chapter list, tick 'Open as a new window' and pick a chapter, or long-press a chapter.
Move freely in the new window, then press Back to return to your main reading.

### How do I search?

Choose search from the menu on the book list screen. Enter at least two letters in Keyword A to find every verse that contains it.
Enter both Keyword A and B to find verses containing both.
Tap a result to open its chapter, or long-press to send it by email or message.
