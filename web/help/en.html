Lifove Bible for the web

A Bible viewer that brings the Android app Lifove Bible to the browser. It uses the same Bible data as the app, and it is free with no ads. Open it in a browser on iPhone, iPad, PC, Mac, or an Android phone or tablet.

  • Change chapters: Swipe the text left or right, or tap the ‹ › buttons. On a computer, the ← → keys also work.
  • Select verses: Tap a verse to select it, and tap again to unselect. Copy or share the selected verses.
  • Highlight: Select verses, then tap Highlight in the strip to color them, or Remove to clear. Each version keeps its own highlights. See them all in ☰ → My Highlight; tap one to go to that verse.
  • Memo: Select one verse, then tap Memo in the strip to open the memo panel at the bottom. Write and tap Save; the memo appears in small text under the verse. You can edit it again or Delete it (saving it empty also deletes it). ☰ → Hide memos shows only ★; tap ★ to open the memo.
  • Bookmark: ☰ → Add Bookmark saves the verse at the top of the screen. In ☰ → Bookmark, tap a saved place to go to that verse.
  • Download: Downloaded versions can be read without an internet connection.
  • Single Viewer · Parallel Viewer: Switch versions or read several side by side.
  • Search: Search with two keywords and a range (all, OT, NT, themes, a book).
  • Add Bible files: Add Bible files you have (.lfa, .bdf).
  • Data Backup: ☰ → Data Management → Proceed Backup downloads your highlights, memos, reading checks, and bookmarks as one file (.db). It is the same backup file as the Android app, so the app can open it in Settings → Restore Data.
  • Restore Data: ☰ → Data Management → Proceed Restore, then pick a backup file. Backups from the Android app work too (.db files in Download/LifoveBible, or lifovebible from older versions). Merge keeps your current records and adds the backup's (you choose for memos that differ); Replace deletes your current records and uses the backup.
  • Viewer Settings: Bar position, text and background colors, navigation buttons.
  • Read aloud: Tap ▶ on the book/chapter bar to hear the current chapter in your device's voice (TTS); at the end it goes on to the next chapter. While reading, tap 1.0x in its place to change the speed, and use the new row on the bar to pause, stop or change chapters. In parallel view, the first version is read. Select a verse first and tap ▶ to start from that verse.

The voice comes from your device, so it differs between devices, and a language without an installed voice cannot be read. The screen is kept on while reading, but reading may stop if you switch apps or turn the screen off.

Installing voices for read aloud

Read aloud uses the voices installed on your device. A web app cannot open your device settings, so tap your device below and follow the steps. After installing, you may need to close the browser (or the installed app) completely and open it again before the new voice appears. Choose a voice in Viewer Settings → Speech (TTS) Settings → Voice.

Android
  1. Install or update Speech Recognition & Synthesis from Google from the Play Store.
  2. In the Settings app, search for Text-to-speech. On Samsung phones it is usually Settings → General management → Text-to-speech.
  3. Choose Google as the preferred engine, then tap ⚙ next to it → Install voice data and download the languages you need.

Chrome only shows the voices of the preferred engine. Voices of the Samsung engine may not be available on the web.

iPhone · iPad
  1. Go to Settings → Accessibility → Read & Speak → Voices. On older iOS it is Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices.
  2. Choose a language and tap the download button next to a voice. “Enhanced” voices sound more natural.
PC · Mac
  • Windows: Settings → Time & language → Speech → Add voices. Edge also shows natural online voices when you are connected.
  • Mac: System Settings → Accessibility → Read & Speak (Spoken Content) → tap ⓘ next to System voice to download voices.
  • Chrome also shows Google voices (need internet) when you are connected.

How to install

You are using the installed app now.

Once installed on your home screen or desktop, it opens from its icon like an app, without the address bar, and works offline. No app store is needed, and it is very small. Tap your device below.

iPhone · iPad

Requires iOS (iPadOS) 16.4 or later.

Safari

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (□↑): at the bottom on iPhone, at the top right of the address bar on iPad. If you don't see it, tap ⋯ next to the address bar, then Share.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen. (If you see Open as Web App, leave it on.)
  4. Tap Add at the top right.

Chrome · Edge · Firefox

  1. Tap the Share button (□↑) at the right of the address bar. In Edge or Firefox it may be inside the menu (⋯ or ≡).
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen → Add.

Please note. On iPhone, browser tabs and home screen apps keep separate storage. Versions and settings from the browser are not in the home screen app, so after installing, open it from the home screen icon and download your versions again in Download. Also, if you only use it in the browser without installing, iPhone may delete downloaded versions after a while of not opening it, so installing is recommended.

Android phone · tablet

On Android, the Lifove Bible app on Google Play has more features (notes, reading marks, read aloud and more). To install the web version:

Chrome

  1. If an Install prompt appears at the bottom, tap it.
  2. Otherwise, tap the menu (⋮) at the top right → Install app or Add to Home screen → Install.

Samsung Internet

  1. If an install icon (↓) appears in the address bar, tap it.
  2. Otherwise, tap the menu (≡) at the bottom → Add page to → Home screen.

Edge · Firefox · others

In the menu (⋯ or ⋮), tap an item like Install app, Install, or Add to Home screen.

PC · Mac · Chromebook

Chrome (Windows, Mac, Linux, Chromebook)

  1. Click the install icon (a monitor with an arrow) at the right end of the address bar, then Install.
  2. If there is no icon, open the menu (⋮) → Cast, save, and share → Install page as app.

Edge (Windows, Mac)

  1. Click the app icon (App available) in the address bar, then Install.
  2. If there is no icon, open the menu (⋯) → Apps → Install this site as an app.

Safari (Mac)

On macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later, choose File → Add to Dock in the menu bar.

Firefox

Firefox cannot install sites as apps. Bookmark it, or use Chrome or Edge to install.

Windows tablets work the same as a PC.

Removing the installed app

  • iPhone · iPad: Touch and hold the icon → Remove App → Delete (or Remove from Home Screen).
  • Android: Touch and hold the icon → Uninstall (or App info → Uninstall).
  • PC: In the app window, open the menu (⋮ or ⋯) at the top right → Uninstall. In Chrome you can also go to chrome://apps.
  • Mac Safari: Drag the icon out of the Dock, or delete it from the Applications folder.

Updates

Nothing to do. When a new version is ready, "A new version is available" appears at the top of the screen. Tap Reload to switch.

Where are my versions and settings?

They are stored in this browser on this device, and are not shared with other devices or browsers. Clearing site data (cookies and site data) in your browser settings removes them too; just download the versions again.

Please back up your records (highlights, memos)

Highlights, memos, bookmarks, and reading records are also stored only in this browser; they are not kept on our server. They can be lost if you clear site data, the device runs low on storage, or you change devices or browsers, and lost records cannot be recovered. Safari on iPhone and iPad may also delete them if the site is not added to the Home Screen and is not opened for more than 7 days.

Please back up regularly in ☰ → Data Management, and keep the files in a safe place such as a computer or a cloud drive. If 7 days pass since your last backup, a reminder appears at the top of the screen (Later reminds you again in 7 days).

If you turn on Google Drive sync (at the top of ☰ → Data Management), your records are also saved in your own Google Drive and you can continue on your other devices. While sync is on and has run within the last 7 days, the backup reminder does not appear. Keeping backup files as well is still safer.

You can use the backup file on other devices too. Move the file by email, messenger, cloud drive, or similar, then pick it in the Lifove Bible app on an Android phone or tablet (Settings → Restore Data), or in the web version on another device or browser (☰ → Data Management → Proceed Restore), to keep using your highlights, memos, and more. Do the same when you change devices.

Making backups and keeping the files is up to you. We cannot restore records that were lost without a backup, and we kindly ask for your understanding that this responsibility rests with you.
